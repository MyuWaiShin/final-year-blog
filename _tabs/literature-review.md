## Robotic Grasping & Tactile Perception

**[1]** Li, T., Yan, Y., Yu, C., An, J., Wang, Y., & Chen, G. (2024). A comprehensive review of robot intelligent grasping based on tactile perception. *Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing*, 90, 102792. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rcim.2024.102792](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rcim.2024.102792)

<details>
<summary>Show abstract</summary>
<p>This paper reviews how robots can use touch sensors (tactile feedback) to improve their grasping abilities. It covers different types of tactile sensors and how combining touch with vision helps robots handle objects more reliably, especially when visual information alone isn't enough.</p>
</details>

## Failure Recovery & Robustness

**[2]** Vats, S., Jha, D. K., Likhachev, M., Kroemer, O., & Romeres, D. RecoveryChaining: Learning Local Recovery Policies for Robust Manipulation. *arXiv preprint*. [https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13979v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13979v1)

<details>
<summary>Show abstract</summary>
<p>This paper presents a reinforcement learning approach where robots learn a chain of recovery actions to handle manipulation failures. They train the system in PyBullet simulation to automatically figure out sequences of corrective actions on three failure types: collision, slips, and position error.</p>
</details>
