Failure Detection and Recovery in Robotic Pick-and-Place Systems

Building intelligent recovery mechanisms for robotic manipulation

What Happens When a Robot Fails?

This research tackles a critical challenge in industrial automation: what happens when a robotic arm fails to grasp an object?

This project investigates intelligent recovery strategies that enable robots to detect failures, understand what went wrong, and autonomously recover, enabling the robot to correctly resume its operation or proceed without a system halt.

UR10 manipulator with gripper and vision system

Why This Matters

In automated manufacturing, every failure that requires human intervention means production delays, increased operational costs, and reduced system reliability. Intelligent recovery mechanisms can significantly improve the autonomy and efficiency of robotic systems.
